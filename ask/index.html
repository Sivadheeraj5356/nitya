Ask Krishna

Ask about life, duty, fear, anger, or the restless mind. A gentle guide answers through the Bhagavad Gita and shows you the verse each answer comes from.

Namaste. I am a humble guide who answers only through the Bhagavad Gita, the song Krishna sang to Arjuna on the field of duty. What weighs on your mind today?

Answers are written by an AI guide grounded in the Bhagavad Gita, with real verse citations. Treat them as study guidance, not the word of a guru, and bring heavy matters to people you trust. Your question is sent to our server to be answered; please do not share personal details.
