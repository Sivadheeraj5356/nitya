Chapter 11 · Yoga through Beholding the Cosmic Form of God

विश्वरूपदर्शनयोग (Viśhwarūp Darśhan Yog) — 55 verses. The eleventh chapter of the Bhagavad Gita is "Vishwaroopa Darshana Yoga". In this chapter, Arjuna requests Krishna to reveal His Universal Cosmic Form that encompasses all the universes, the entire existence. Arjuna is granted divine vision to be able to see the entirety of creation in the body of the Supreme Lord Krishna.
