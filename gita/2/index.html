Chapter 2 · Transcendental Knowledge

सांख्ययोग (Sānkhya Yog) — 72 verses. The second chapter of the Bhagavad Gita is "Sankhya Yoga". This is the most important chapter of the Bhagavad Gita as Lord Krishna condenses the teachings of the entire Gita in this chapter. This chapter is the essence of the entire Gita.
