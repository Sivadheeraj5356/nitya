Mantra library

Twelve foundational mantras from the Vedas and Upanishads, with the text, transliteration, meaning and source. Read slowly; chant with the mala.

Gayatri Mantra

Rig Veda 3.62.10
ॐ भूर्भुवः स्वः। तत्सवितुर्वरेण्यं। भर्गो देवस्य धीमहि। धियो यो नः प्रचोदयात्॥
oṃ bhūr bhuvaḥ svaḥ tat savitur vareṇyaṃ bhargo devasya dhīmahi dhiyo yo naḥ prachodayāt

We meditate on the divine light of the Sun, the source of all realms — earth, sky and heavens. May it illumine and guide our understanding. The oldest and most revered prayer of the Vedas, chanted at dawn.

Mahā Mṛityuñjaya Mantra

Rig Veda 7.59.12
ॐ त्र्यम्बकं यजामहे सुगन्धिं पुष्टिवर्धनम्। उर्वारुकमिव बन्धनान्मृत्योर्मुक्षीय मामृतात्॥
oṃ tryambakaṃ yajāmahe sugandhiṃ puṣṭi-vardhanam urvārukam iva bandhanān mṛityor mukṣhīya māmṛitāt

We worship the three-eyed Lord, fragrant and nourishing. As a ripe cucumber frees itself from its stem, may He free us from death — not from immortality. Chanted for healing, protection and courage in difficulty.

Om Namaḥ Śivāya

Śrī Rudram, Yajurveda
ॐ नमः शिवाय।
oṃ namaḥ śivāya

I bow to Shiva — the auspicious one within. The five-syllable Panchakshara mantra, the heart of Shaiva practice, repeated for purification and steadiness of mind.

Hare Kṛiṣhṇa Mahāmantra

Kali Santarana Upanishad
हरे कृष्ण हरे कृष्ण। कृष्ण कृष्ण हरे हरे। हरे राम हरे राम। राम राम हरे हरे॥
hare kṛiṣhṇa hare kṛiṣhṇa kṛiṣhṇa kṛiṣhṇa hare hare hare rāma hare rāma rāma rāma hare hare

O Lord Krishna, O Lord Rama, O divine energy — please engage me in Your remembrance. The great mantra of the Bhakti tradition, chanted as the simplest path in this age.

Gāṇeśha Mantra

Ganesha Purana tradition
वक्रतुण्ड महाकाय सूर्यकोटि समप्रभ। निर्विघ्नं कुरु मे देव सर्वकार्येषु सर्वदा॥
vakratuṇḍa mahākāya sūryakoṭi samaprabha nirvighnaṃ kuru me deva sarva-kāryeṣhu sarvadā

O Lord with the curved trunk and mighty form, radiant as a million suns — remove every obstacle from my undertakings, always. Spoken at the start of any new work.

Śānti Mantra

Taittiriya Upanishad
ॐ सह नाववतु। सह नौ भुनक्तु। सह वीर्यं करवावहै। तेजस्विनावधीतमस्तु मा विद्विषावहै॥ ॐ शान्तिः शान्तिः शान्तिः॥
oṃ saha nāvavatu saha nau bhunaktu saha vīryaṃ karavāvahai tejasvi nāvadhītam astu mā vidviṣhāvahai oṃ śāntiḥ śāntiḥ śāntiḥ

May we be protected together, nourished together, and work with shared strength. May our study be luminous and free of discord. Peace — in body, in world, in spirit. Chanted before study.

Pūrṇam Adaḥ

Isha Upanishad invocation
ॐ पूर्णमदः पूर्णमिदं। पूर्णात्पूर्णमुदच्यते। पूर्णस्य पूर्णमादाय। पूर्णमेवावशिष्यते॥
oṃ pūrṇam adaḥ pūrṇam idaṃ pūrṇāt pūrṇam udachyate pūrṇasya pūrṇam ādāya pūrṇam evāvaśhiṣhyate

That is whole; this is whole. From wholeness, wholeness arises. Take wholeness from wholeness, and wholeness alone remains. A meditation on the completeness of the Self.

Asato Mā Sad Gamaya

Brihadaranyaka Upanishad 1.3.28
ॐ असतो मा सद्गमय। तमसो मा ज्योतिर्गमय। मृत्योर्मा अमृतं गमय॥
oṃ asato mā sad gamaya tamaso mā jyotir gamaya mṛityor mā amṛitaṃ gamaya

Lead me from the unreal to the real, from darkness to light, from death to immortality. The Upanishadic prayer for truth, clarity and fearlessness.

Tvameva Mātā

Pandava Gita tradition
त्वमेव माता च पिता त्वमेव। त्वमेव बन्धुश्च सखा त्वमेव। त्वमेव विद्या द्रविणं त्वमेव। त्वमेव सर्वं मम देव देव॥
tvameva mātā cha pitā tvameva tvameva bandhuśh cha sakhā tvameva tvameva vidyā draviṇaṃ tvameva tvameva sarvaṃ mama deva deva

You alone are my mother and father, my family and my friend, my knowledge and my wealth. You are everything to me, O God of gods. A prayer of complete trust.

Guru Mantra

Vishvasara Tantra
गुरुर्ब्रह्मा गुरुर्विष्णुः। गुरुर्देवो महेश्वरः। गुरुः साक्षात्परं ब्रह्म। तस्मै श्रीगुरवे नमः॥
gurur brahmā gurur viṣhṇuḥ gurur devo maheśhvaraḥ guruḥ sākṣhāt paraṃ brahma tasmai śhrī gurave namaḥ

The guru is Brahma the creator, Vishnu the sustainer, Shiva the transformer — the guru is the absolute itself. Salutations to that teacher. Honouring the lineage of wisdom.

Karāgre Vasate Lakṣhmī

Morning prayer
कराग्रे वसते लक्ष्मीः। करमध्ये सरस्वती। करमूले तु गोविन्दः। प्रभाते करदर्शनम्॥
karāgre vasate lakṣhmīḥ karamadhye sarasvatī karamūle tu govindaḥ prabhāte karadarśhanam

At the fingertips dwells Lakshmi, in the palm Saraswati, at the base of the hand Govinda. To look upon one's hands at dawn is to remember that the day's work is sacred.

Sarveṣhāṃ Svastir Bhavatu

Universal shanti prayer
ॐ सर्वेषां स्वस्तिर्भवतु। सर्वेषां शान्तिर्भवतु। सर्वेषां पूर्णं भवतु। सर्वेषां मङ्गलं भवतु॥
oṃ sarveṣhāṃ svastir bhavatu sarveṣhāṃ śhāntir bhavatu sarveṣhāṃ pūrṇaṃ bhavatu sarveṣhāṃ maṅgalaṃ bhavatu

May all beings be well, may all be at peace, may all be whole, may all be auspicious. A blessing for the world, closing any practice.
