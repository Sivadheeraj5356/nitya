Sankashti Chaturthi Dates 2026-2027 · सङ्कटहरचतुर्थी

Sankashti Chaturthi, the fourth day of the waning moon, is Ganesha's monthly vrata, broken after moonrise. Dates below are for New Delhi; when it falls on a Tuesday it is the especially auspicious Angaraki Chaturthi.
