Amavasya Dates 2026-2027 · अमावस्या

Amavasya, the new-moon day of each lunar month, is set aside for tarpana to the pitrs and for quiet japa. Dates below are for New Delhi, with the month names used in the North Indian (purnimanta) calendar.
