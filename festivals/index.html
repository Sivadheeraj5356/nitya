Festival calendar 2026-2027 · पर्वाणि

Festival and vrata dates computed from the astronomical positions of the sun and moon (Swiss Ephemeris, Lahiri ayanamsa) for New Delhi, following the traditional paraviddha-purvaviddha rules - the same method behind the national drik panchang.

Diwali (Deepavali)
दीपावली
The festival of lights, celebrating the victory of light over darkness.
Next: 8 November 2026
Holi
होली
The spring festival of colours.
Next: 22 March 2027
Maha Shivaratri
महाशिवरात्रिः
The great night of Shiva, observed with fasting, night-long vigil and abhishekam of the Shiva linga.
Next: 6 March 2027
Ram Navami
श्रीरामनवमी
The appearance day of Bhagavan Sri Rama, celebrated on the ninth day of the bright half of Chaitra, at madhyahna (midday).
Next: 15 April 2027
Hanuman Jayanti
हनुमज्जयन्ती
The appearance day of Hanuman.
Next: 20 April 2027
Makar Sankranti / Pongal
मकरसङ्क्रमणम्
The sun enters Makara (Capricorn) and begins Uttarayana, its northward course.
Next: 15 January 2027
Jagannath Rath Yatra
जगन्नाथरथयात्रा
The chariot festival of Jagannath, Balabhadra and Subhadra at Puri, on the second day of the bright half of Ashadha.
Next: 5 July 2027
Guru Purnima (Vyasa Puja)
गुरुपूर्णिमा
The full moon of Ashadha, dedicated to the guru and to Veda Vyasa, who compiled the Vedas on this day.
Next: 18 July 2027
Raksha Bandhan
रक्षाबन्धनम्
The festival of the bond between brothers and sisters, when sisters tie the rakhi.
Next: 17 August 2027
Krishna Janmashtami
श्रीकृष्णजन्माष्टमी
The appearance of Bhagavan Sri Krishna, observed on the eighth night of the waning moon of Bhadrapada (Shravana in the amanta reckoning), with the birth celebrated at midnight (nishita kaal).
Next: 24 August 2027
Ganesh Chaturthi
श्रीविनायकचतुर्थी
The appearance day of Ganesha, worshipped as Vighneshvara, the remover of obstacles, on the fourth day of the bright half of Bhadrapada, at madhyahna.
Next: 4 September 2027
Sharad Navratri and Dussehra
शरन्नवरात्रम् / विजयदशमी
Nine nights of the worship of Devi in her nine forms, culminating in Durga Ashtami, Maha Navami and Vijayadashami (Dussehra), the victory of Rama over Ravana and of Devi over Mahishasura.
Next: 10 October 2027
Onam
ओणम्
Kerala's harvest festival, welcoming the annual visit of King Mahabali, on the Thiruvonam (Shravana) nakshatra of Chingam month.
Next: 12 September 2027
Akshaya Tritiya
अक्षयतृतीया
The "never-diminishing" third day of Vaishakha, considered auspicious for beginnings, charity and worship without needing a muhurta.
Next: 9 May 2027
Varalakshmi Vratam
वरलक्ष्मीव्रतम्
The worship of Varalakshmi, the boon-granting form of Lakshmi, kept by married women on the Friday before Shravana Purnima.
Next: 13 August 2027

Monthly observances

Ekadashi Dates 2026-2027
एकादशी
Ekadashi, the eleventh tithi of each fortnight, is Vishnu's own day, kept with fasting and japa.
Amavasya Dates 2026-2027
अमावस्या
Amavasya, the new-moon day of each lunar month, is set aside for tarpana to the pitrs and for quiet japa.
Purnima Dates 2026-2027
पूर्णिमा
Purnima, the full-moon day, is auspicious for Satyanarayana puja, vrata and daana.
Pradosha Dates 2026-2027
प्रदोषव्रतम्
Pradosha is the worship of Shiva at dusk on the trayodashi of both fortnights.
Sankashti Chaturthi Dates 2026-2027
सङ्कटहरचतुर्थी
Sankashti Chaturthi, the fourth day of the waning moon, is Ganesha's monthly vrata, broken after moonrise.
Masik Shivaratri Dates 2026-2027
मासशिवरात्रिः
Masik Shivaratri, the monthly night of Shiva, falls on the fourteenth tithi of every waning fortnight.

Tithis begin and end at their own times of day, so a festival can fall on different dates in different places. These dates are computed for New Delhi (IST); for other cities they can shift by a day in rare cases.
