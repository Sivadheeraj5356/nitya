Sharad Navratri and Dussehra · शरन्नवरात्रम् / विजयदशमी

Dates for 2026 and 2027, computed astronomically for New Delhi (IST).

Nine nights of the worship of Devi in her nine forms, culminating in Durga Ashtami, Maha Navami and Vijayadashami (Dussehra), the victory of Rama over Ravana and of Devi over Mahishasura.
